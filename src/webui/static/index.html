    Results

    Per-model accuracy over the targets that are present on both the baseline and the profile being compared. Step through any model's misses to see what it actually pointed at.

    Analyze

    Reachability, the pooled permutation test across models, per-model McNemar tests, and direction consistency. Vision and tree results are analysed separately and never pooled.
